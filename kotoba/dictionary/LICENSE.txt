2. Tomoshi Open Dictionary Data — AI-assisted Chinese definitions
Chinese derivative content copyright (c) Y1Z, distributed by Tomoshi.
Underlying dictionary content: JMdict / EDRDG, attributed above.
Project and licence: https://github.com/tomoshi-app/tomoshi-dict-data
Release used: https://github.com/tomoshi-app/tomoshi-dict-data/releases/tag/v2026-10-13
The release database identifies entries and zh_defs as CC-BY-SA-4.0.

These Chinese definitions are generated and reviewed using language models;
they are not publisher-edited definitions. Each result labels this source as
"Tomoshi · AI 辅助释义". Only zh-CN glosses with a matching JMdict ID, overlapping
Japanese spelling/reading and unambiguous identical English sense glosses are
included. The current JMdict sense index is retained. Database examples, orphan
example translations, proprietary app content and unrelated tables are excluded.
The application invokes no model to create or translate dictionary results.
Modifications: selected glosses, sense alignment, compact JSON and gzip.

3. Chinese Wiktionary — Chinese definitions of Japanese entries
Copyright the Wiktionary contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0.
Original: https://zh.wiktionary.org/
Contributor and revision history: each result links its original wiki page;
select that page's history to view its authors and revisions.
Terms: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use
Extraction: Kaikki.org / Wiktextract, Tatu Ylonen and contributors.
Extraction source: https://kaikki.org/zhwiktionary/rawdata.html
Raw data: https://kaikki.org/zhwiktionary/raw-wiktextract-data.jsonl.gz
Wiktionary dump snapshot: 2026-10-01 (extraction: 2026-10-02).

Modifications: Japanese-language records filtered; exact duplicates,
romanizations and empty definitions omitted. Original glosses, usage tags and
up to two examples without external quotation references per sense retained.
Canonical ruby converted to kana readings. No audio files are redistributed.
A record is linked to JMdict only if its spelling and all known readings match
one unambiguous entry and comply with reading restrictions. Other records remain
independent. Wiktionary senses are never aligned to JMdict sense numbers.
Data is compacted to JSON and gzip; wording and simplified/traditional forms
remain as supplied by the source. Page links provide attribution and revisions.

The three sources overlap; their counts must not be added as unique vocabulary.

compatible licence; attribution and licence links must be preserved. These data
licences do not change the separate licensing of app code or original lessons.
