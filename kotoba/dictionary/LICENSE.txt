2. Tomoshi Open Dictionary Data — AI-assisted Chinese definitions
Chinese derivative content copyright (c) Y1Z, distributed by Tomoshi.
Underlying dictionary content: JMdict / EDRDG, attributed above.
Project and licence: https://github.com/tomoshi-app/tomoshi-dict-data
Release used: https://github.com/tomoshi-app/tomoshi-dict-data/releases/tag/v2026-10-13
The release database identifies entries and zh_defs as CC-BY-SA-4.0.

These Chinese definitions are generated and reviewed using language models;
they are not publisher-edited definitions. Each result labels this source as
"Tomoshi · AI 辅助释义". Only zh-CN glosses with a matching JMdict ID, overlapping
Japanese spelling/reading and unambiguous identical English sense glosses are
included. The current JMdict sense index is retained. Database examples, orphan
example translations, proprietary app content and unrelated tables are excluded.
The application invokes no model to create or translate dictionary results.
Modifications: selected glosses, sense alignment, compact JSON and gzip.

3. Chinese Wiktionary — Chinese definitions of Japanese entries
Copyright the Wiktionary contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0.
Original: https://zh.wiktionary.org/
Contributor and revision history: each result links its original wiki page;
select that page's history to view its authors and revisions.
Terms: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use
Extraction: Kaikki.org / Wiktextract, Tatu Ylonen and contributors.
Extraction source: https://kaikki.org/zhwiktionary/rawdata.html
Raw data: https://kaikki.org/zhwiktionary/raw-wiktextract-data.jsonl.gz
Wiktionary dump snapshot: 2026-10-01 (extraction: 2026-10-02).

Modifications: Japanese-language records filtered; exact duplicates,
romanizations and empty definitions omitted. Original glosses, usage tags and
up to two examples without external quotation references per sense retained.
Canonical ruby converted to kana readings. No audio files are redistributed.
A record is linked to JMdict only if its spelling and all known readings match
one unambiguous entry and comply with reading restrictions. Other records remain
independent. Wiktionary senses are never aligned to JMdict sense numbers.
Data is compacted to JSON and gzip; wording and simplified/traditional forms
remain as supplied by the source. Page links provide attribution and revisions.

4. Kanjium and Wiktionary — pitch-accent supplement
Kanjium pitch-accent data copyright (c) Uros O. / Kanjium contributors.
Project, attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 licence:
https://github.com/mifunetoshiro/kanjium
Source: data/source_files/raw/accents.txt (retrieved 2026-10-13).
Wiktionary contributor attribution, dump snapshot and licence: section 3 above.

Modifications: compact JSON and gzip; kana normalized; spelling, reading and
JMdict reading restrictions matched to one unambiguous entry. Original Kanjium
alternatives and usage conditions are retained. Tokyo pitch notation is taken
from the same Wiktionary snapshot as the definitions and matched to the exact
Wiktionary record/reading. Only unambiguous mora-aligned notation is converted to
numeric pitch positions; other matched notation is preserved verbatim. Missing
or ambiguous matches are omitted. Diagrams are drawn from supplied positions,
not inferred from spelling, generated speech or a language model. Sources and
spelling conditions are displayed with each pattern. No audio is redistributed.

Source: https://www.edrdg.org/pub/Nihongo/JMdict_e_examp.gz (2026-10-13).
Sentence-dictionary linking: https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/Sentence-Dictionary_Linking.html
Sentence text copyright the respective Tatoeba contributors, credited per

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/legalcode
Source and download conditions: https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads
Detailed Japanese, English and Mandarin sentence exports and direct Japanese–
English / Japanese–Mandarin links: 2026-10-10 snapshot. Each file hash is in the
manifest. Sentences without a current owner are credited to Tatoeba contributors;
the linked original sentence page provides its contribution history.

Modifications: select unambiguous JMdict entries/senses with unchanged writings,
readings, English glosses and sense restrictions. Require current Japanese text
to equal the linked JMdict example and require its original English sentence to
be an unchanged direct Tatoeba translation. Attach up to two direct Mandarin
translations, retain original text/author/ID, prioritize Chinese availability,
and keep up to three examples per sense. No pivot-based, generated or machine-
translated Chinese is added. Missing Chinese translations are explicitly marked.
Encoding changed to compact JSON and gzip; keyword highlighting does not change
sentence wording. No recordings are redistributed. The sentence text retains
CC BY 2.0 FR; the separate JMdict associations retain CC BY-SA 4.0.
